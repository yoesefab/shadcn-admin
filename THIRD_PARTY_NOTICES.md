## Date Range Picker for shadcn

`src/components/date-range-picker.tsx` and `src/components/date-input.tsx`
adapt the interaction and component structure of
[John Polacek’s Date Range Picker for shadcn](https://github.com/johnpolacek/date-range-picker-for-shadcn)

the project’s native shadcn components, and French date entry.
